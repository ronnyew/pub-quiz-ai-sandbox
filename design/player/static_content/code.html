Information We Collect

When you register, authenticate via FirebaseUI, or play in live rounds, we collect:

  • Authentication Data: Email addresses, screen names, and OAuth tokens passed through third-party identity providers (Google, Phone/SMS).
  • Gameplay Telemetry: Answer submission response times, round points, room connection status, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Invitation Dispatches: Phone numbers or email addresses provided solely to transmit one-time game room join links.

NOTE: INVITATION DISPATCHES ARE PURGED 48 HOURS AFTER GAME ROOM CLOSURE.
